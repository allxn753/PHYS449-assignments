(a) Begin with a basis set up to linear order in $T$, and find the regression solution. Calculate the mean squared error (MSE) on both the training and the validation set. Discuss.

The regression line would take the form of:

$$E(T) \approx w_0 + w_1T$$

Where $w_0$ and $w_1$ are the weights of the model. $w_0$ is the extrapolated energy at $T=0$ and $w_1$ is the change in energy per spin per unit temperature (averaged over $T \in [1.0, 1.5]$). To get $w$, we solve the normal equation:

$$X^\top Xw=X^\top y \Rightarrow w=(X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top y$$

The mean squared error (MSE) for the training data would be:

$$MSE_{training}=\frac{1}{N_{training}} \sum(E-\hat E)^2$$

Similarly, the MSE for the validation data would be:

$$MSE_{validation}=\frac{1}{N_{validation}} \sum(E-\hat E)^2$$
